# Game illustration atlas

`game-atlas-v2.png` was generated with the built-in image_gen tool. It is a transparent 1536 × 1024 PNG, arranged as a 3 × 2 grid and consumed without bitmap modification by GameSprite.vue.

Final prompt:

Use case: stylized-concept. Create a production transparent PNG sprite atlas for a children's rock paper scissors game, 3 columns x 2 rows of equal square cells, total aspect 3:2. Each isolated item centered in its own cell with 12% transparent padding, no overlap. Top row: front-facing golden yellow closed fist with thumb folded across curled fingers; golden yellow scissors hand with ONLY index and middle extended in V and ring/little curled under thumb; golden yellow open palm all FIVE fingers visible. Bottom row: adorable white and lavender robot dog HEAD with floppy ears and shiny black face screen and cyan happy eyes; cheerful cute brown-haired boy HEAD; golden jeweled crown. Glossy rounded 3D toy illustration, orange contours for hands, soft gradient highlights, warm friendly expressive shapes, matching high-end pastel children's game art. Transparent background actual alpha, NO white rectangle, NO text, NO labels, NO outlines around cells, no circles, no card UI. Six sprites precisely centered on regular grid for CSS atlas extraction. The hands must be anatomically unambiguous and rendered straight-on with no wrist extending below. No watermark.
